Good to know

Can I cancel?

Cancel at least 7 days before check-in for a full refund of all nights, minus a 5% processing fee. Each home shows its exact policy before you book, and travel insurance is recommended.

Can we bring our dog?

Yes. Every SA Keys home accepts pets. Let the host know before you arrive; a pet cleaning fee may apply.

Are parties allowed?

No parties or unapproved events. Small gatherings can be approved by the host before you book. Some homes have sound monitors to keep noise down for the neighbors.

How do I get in?

Contactless self check-in. The host sends the door code and directions before your stay.
